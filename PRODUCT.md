# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Government agencies, real estate developers, industrial contractors, infrastructure authorities, and environmental management bodies seeking top-tier civil and environmental engineering consulting, feasibility studies, road network design, flood mitigation, and geotechnical analysis.

## Product Purpose

Enviro Civec (إنفيرو سيفيك) is a specialized engineering consultancy platform showcasing high-impact infrastructure achievements, certified multidisciplinary consulting services, and converting corporate & municipal prospects through an inquiry workflow.

## Positioning

Bridging advanced civil infrastructure engineering with ecological preservation and sustainable design methodologies across the MENA region, supported by ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001 certified engineering processes.

## Operating Context

Evaluated by C-level executives, municipal procurement directors, and chief engineers on both desktop (office workstation with CAD-like precision) and high-end mobile devices in bilingual contexts (Arabic & English).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dual-language engine with complete RTL/LTR parity (Arabic / English).
- High-performance bespoke AutoCAD cursor experience with precision crosshair and window/crossing selections.
- Multi-section architectural narrative: Hero, About, Key Stats, Services Showcase with detail capabilities, Featured Project Portfolio, Testimonials, Partners, and Secure Contact Form.
- Light/Dark theme switching with persistent client state.
- Ultra-secure contact endpoint with anti-spam honeypot and rate limiting.
